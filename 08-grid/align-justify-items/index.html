In this example, we demonstrate the use of align-items, justify-items, align-self, and justify-self to control the alignment of grid items within their grid areas. Item 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, and 9 are aligned to the center of their grid areas because they follow the alignment and justification of the grid container that is set to align-items: center; and justify-items: center;. The other items have their own alignment and justification set using align-self and justify-self.
